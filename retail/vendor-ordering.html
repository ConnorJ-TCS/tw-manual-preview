Vendor Ordering

If your store is set up to order electronically from a vendor, TireWorks HD can show the vendor's quantity and price during searches, send purchase orders straight to the vendor, and build replenishment orders on a schedule. Your TCS representative or administrator sets up each connection.

Set up a vendor for electronic ordering

  1. Go to Manage; Company Setup; Vendors.
    • To list only vendors with an online connection, check Show electronic vendors only.
  • Vendor Type: Identifies which ordering system the vendor uses. Types include Michelin BIB, Michelin BWS, TCS Partner, ATD, JW Tires, MaxFinkelstein, Bridgestone Treadnet and OpenWebs. Vendor types beginning with VIP are for VIP-owned inventory.
  • Capabilities: Shows what the connection supports: Qty., Prices, Order and Replenish. Select Refresh Capabilities after a change.
  • Map SubMfrs for Lookup: Controls which brands the vendor is checked for in searches. Map SubMfrs For Replenish is covered under Vendor Replenishment.
  • Max Items: Limits how many items can go on one purchase order to the vendor.

Note: Parts suppliers reached through a parts network (for example ISE or IAP sellers, or a direct seller such as O'Reilly) are set up under Manage; Company Setup; Supplier/IAP Seller/Direct Seller and Epicor ISE Configuration.

Check vendor availability in searches

  • Order-capable vendors: Show their quantity and price in the F1 Tire Search results, beside your own inventory and your other stores (ASL).
  • Parts suppliers: Show quantity and price on F3 Parts, Parts Resolution, Quick Quote and Appointments.

Send a purchase order to the vendor

  1. Create the purchase order as usual, then select Send PO.
    • Send PO works only for a vendor that can take orders. If the vendor needs extra details (Vendor Requirements), you're asked to fill them in first.
    • TireWorks HD checks availability with the vendor, then shows Availability Results with PO Qty., PO Cost, Vendor Qty. Avail. and Vendor Cost for each line.
      • Green check: Item available for order.
      • Yellow triangle: Insufficient quantity at supplier, with the quantity available.
      • Red exclamation: Item not found at supplier, or Item found but unavailable.
  2. Review the results, then select Continue to send or Cancel to stop.

Note: Your administrator chooses the order number your vendor sees on online orders from the Sourcing Manager: the service request number, the purchase order number, or both (SR, PO or PO-SR).

Automatic replenishment orders

  • If your store has a Replenishment Schedule for a vendor (see Vendor Replenishment), TireWorks HD creates and sends replenishment purchase orders on that schedule, in the store's local time.
  • A replenishment purchase order that can't be sent to the vendor is cancelled automatically, with a comment explaining why.
  • Each run sends an internal text alert to the store for every purchase order or transfer it creates or fails to create. Alerts arrive in the SMS Hub. See Customer Communication (Texting).
