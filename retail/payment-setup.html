Payment Setup

Set up payment terms in Manage; Company Setup; Payment Terms. Set up credit card terminals in Manage; Location Setup; Credit Card Terminals. See Electronic Payments.

Set up payment terms

The payment terms you set up here are the choices in Payment Terms on customers (General tab), customer classes, purchase orders, inventory receipts and AP invoices.

Before you start: Saving changes requires the Manage - Payment Terms access right. Without it, "You do not have permission to save data." displays.

  1. Go to Manage; Company Setup; Payment Terms.
    • The list shows each Payment Term Code. Use Look For to filter by Terms Code.
  2. Select Add New Payment Term.
  3. Enter the Terms Code Id and Terms Code Display. Both are required, and each Terms Code Id can be used only once.
  4. Complete any other fields your business uses: Due Type, Due Date Days, Disc. Type, Disc. Date Days, Disc. Calc. Type, Disc. Amount, Disc. Percent, Sales Purchase, Disc. CB, Freight, Misc, Tax, Proxy Day, Proxy Day Disc., Term 1 through Term 4, Cutoff Day and Payment Type.
  5. Select Save. "Save Successful" displays.
    • If your company uses accounting integration, saved terms are also sent to your accounting package.

Delete a payment term

  1. Select the term.
  2. Select the delete icon.
  3. Select Yes at "Are you sure to delete this Payment Term?"

Note: A term that is already in use can't be deleted. A message explains why.

How payment terms set AP invoice due dates

When an AP invoice uses a term with a Proxy Day, its due date falls on that day of the month. Due Date Days sets how far out, counted in 30-day months. An invoice dated on or after the Cutoff Day moves one more month out.
