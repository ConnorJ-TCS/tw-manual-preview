Customer Approval

Use Customer Approval to have the customer authorize a Service Request before you save, print, or invoice it at the order states your company chose. The customer can sign in the store, or you can record how they approved when they aren't there.

Set up Customer Approval

  1. Go to Manage; Location Setup; Locations, and open the General tab.
  2. Check Signature Capture to turn Customer Approval on for the store.
  3. Go to Manage; Company Setup; Company, and open the Signature Capture tab.
  4. Move each order state that should ask for approval from All SR Order States to Enabled SR Order States with the > button.
    • Use < to remove a state.

Note: Both parts of the setup are needed. If the store box is checked but no order states are listed, the Customer Approval prompt never appears.

Get the customer's approval

When you save, print, or invoice at a listed order state, the Customer Approval prompt asks "Approve via signature?" Choose one of these options:

  • OK: The customer signs on the signature pad, and you select Save, or Clear to start over. If your store's credit card terminal supports signatures, the customer signs on the terminal instead.
  • Customer Not Present: Use this when the customer isn't in the store. At Select Customer Approval Reason, choose how the customer approved in Select Approval Reason. Complete Authorized By, Authorized Contact, and Comments/Notes as needed, then select Save.
  • Text To Authorize: Texts the customer a link. Pick the customer's cell number in Authorized Contact and select Send. The customer views the Service Request on their phone and signs there.
  • Not Now: Closes the prompt without approving. The order doesn't finish saving or invoicing.

Note: Customer Not Present covers only the current session on that order. It isn't offered at invoicing when Get Approval is turned on and Invoice is a listed order state.

Note: Text To Authorize shows only when the System Setting "Merchant Partners - Text to Authorize Enabled" is on.

When the customer is asked to sign again

Once signed, the order doesn't ask again unless the total goes above the amount the customer signed for.

A lower total doesn't ask again. Neither does a higher total caused only by removing a discount that was already on the signed order.

Reprinting an invoice that already printed doesn't ask for a signature.

Record approval for added work (Get Approval)

Stores with Get Approval turned on see a Get Approval button on the Service Request. Use it to record how the customer approved added work.

  1. Select Get Approval.
  2. Complete the Select Customer Approval Reason window, including Additional Service and Additional Amount.
  3. Select Save.

Note: When the total changes after the estimate, the order can also ask for an approval reason on save. The first time, it may ask "Is $[total] the final initial estimate?"

Approval column on the Home screen

The Approval column on the Service Request tab shows Text To Authorize links only:

  • Clock: "Pending Customer Action for Approval".
  • Check mark: The customer signed the link.
  • Blank: No link was sent.

Note: A phone, email, or Customer Not Present approval doesn't change this column.

You also get a text message in the store's text message portal when the customer signs.
