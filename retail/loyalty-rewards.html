Loyalty Rewards

Use Loyalty Rewards to see a customer's rewards on a service request, enroll customers, and redeem rewards on the order. This applies when your company uses a loyalty program (Clutch or BayIQ). Your company uses one program at a time.

The Rewards bubble

The Loyalty Rewards bubble shows at the bottom of the service request once a customer (not the default customer) is on the order.

  • Gift icon: Orange when the customer isn't enrolled, and green when enrolled.
  • Count: How many rewards the customer has available.
  • Applied: The amount of rewards already applied to the order.

Select the bubble to open the Loyalty Rewards window.

Note: If the reward service can't be reached, the window shows "WebService Unreachable!". Select Retry Now.

Enroll a customer

Before you start: Enrolling a new customer requires the Loyalty Rewards - Enroll Button access right. Updating an enrolled customer doesn't.

  1. In the Loyalty Rewards window, select Enroll. The button reads Update once the customer is enrolled.
    • Clutch: If a loyalty customer already exists with the same phone or email, they're listed. Select one and select Done to use it, or select SignUp New to enroll.
    • BayIQ: In the BayiQ Account Selector, search by last name, email, phone, or card number, then select Done or Sign Up.

Note: Depending on your company's setup, you may be prompted, or required, to enter the customer's email before enrolling.

Tip: Your store can use Order Validation (Reward Program Enrollment) to warn when the customer on an order isn't enrolled.

Redeem rewards

  1. Open the Balances tab.
    • It shows Points and the rewards available, with Amount, Description, and Expiry.
  2. Select a reward, then select Redeem to apply it to the order.
    • BayIQ rewards with no set value can be redeemed for the amount you enter. Others are limited to their value.
    • Clutch: One reward and one coupon can be applied to an order, unless your company allows multiple cashback rewards.

Note: Rewards used on an order are removed from the customer's available list once the order is invoiced.

Note: Rewards can't exceed the order total. If they would result in change due to the customer, Order Validation stops the order until the rewards or the order are adjusted.
