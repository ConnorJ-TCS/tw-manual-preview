Electronic Payments

Use electronic payments to take card payments on Service Requests, Customer Deposits, and Receipts On Account. A store takes card payments through one of two processors, depending on how the store is set up: 1stMile (also called Merchant Partners) or iPOSPays. A store uses one or the other, not both. Card payments work the same way on all three order types. The differences between the processors are described below.

What differs between the two processors

  • 1stMile: The 1stMile window opens on your computer to take the card. When the store is set up for it, 1stMile also handles checks, plus Text-to-Pay and Cards on File.
  • iPOSPays: A standalone card terminal takes the card. The terminal doesn't need to be connected to your computer. A "Connecting to credit card terminal..." message shows while TireWorks HD waits on the terminal. iPOSPays offers cash discounting. It doesn't offer Text-to-Pay, Cards on File, or electronic check sales.

Select a credit card terminal (iPOSPays)

When you log in at a store that uses iPOSPays, the Credit Card Terminals window asks you to "Select credit card terminal for this session:".

  1. Choose the terminal at your counter.
  2. To skip this prompt the next time you log in to this location, check Do not ask again.
    • This checkbox only shows for users with the Access Right Credit Card Terminal "Do Not Ask Again" Selection.
  3. Select Done.

Change your credit card terminal

  1. Go to TireWorks; My Preferences, and open the Credit Card Terminal tab.
  2. Pick a new terminal from Credit Card Terminal Name, or clear Do Not Ask Again On Location Selection.
  3. Select Test Status to confirm the terminal is online.

Note: Managers add terminals in Location Setup; Credit Card Terminals, with a Terminal Name, Terminal Profile Number (TPN), and Authorization Key.

Take a card payment

  1. In Add Payment, select Credit Card in Payment Type.
  2. Enter the Payment Amount.
  3. Select Apply.
  4. Complete the card payment.
    • 1stMile: Complete the card in the 1stMile window.
    • iPOSPays: The customer taps, inserts, or swipes on the terminal.

Note: With 1stMile, when the store is set up for checks, choosing Check opens the 1stMile Check Sale screen.

Note: With iPOSPays, if cash discounting is turned on for your store and the customer chooses cash on the terminal, the store's cash discount is added to the Service Request. Cash discounting isn't allowed on Customer Deposits or Receipts On Account. On those, retry and don't pick the cash option.

If an electronic payment fails

A failed card charge shows "Credit Card NOT charged!!" and asks "Do you want to enter a Credit Card transaction manually?"

  • Yes: Type in the card details for a charge that was run another way.
  • No: Go back and try again.

A failed check shows "Check sale NOT processed!!!" and offers to enter the check manually.

Note: The manual option only shows for users with the Access Right Payments - Manual Credit Card. Without it, you're told you don't have access and returned to the payment screen.

Save a card on file (1stMile)

When 1stMile asks "Tokenize this card?", answer Yes to save the card to the bill-to customer.

Saved cards show on the customer's Cards On File tab, with the Card Number, Card Type, and Security Token.

Important: To remove a saved card, hover over the card, select the Delete icon, and confirm. This can't be undone.

Charge a saved card (1stMile)

  1. In Payment Type, choose Credit Card on File.
  2. Select the card from the list.

Note: Credit Card on File only shows when the bill-to customer has a saved card.

Note: If the charge fails, "Credit Card on File NOT charged!!" returns you to the payment screen. There's no manual option for a saved card.

Send a Text-to-Pay link (1stMile)

  1. On a saved Service Request, select the Text To Pay icon next to Add Payment.
  2. Under Create New PayLink, enter the Amount. It can't be more than the remaining balance.
  3. Enter the Phone.
    • The customer's default text-enabled contact fills in first. You can send to another number.
  4. Select Send.

The TextToPay Links list shows each link's Amount and Status. From the list, you can use these buttons:

  • Delete: Cancels the link.
  • Resend: Sends the link again.
  • Accept: Applies a paid link to the order.

Note: Paid links are checked about every five minutes. The store gets a text when a customer pays, and the Service Request list shows a partially or fully paid icon.

Voids, Refunds, and Reversals

  • Removing a card payment: Within about 8 minutes, the payment is voided. After that, it's refunded to the card.
  • Reversals (Return Order): Your store may ask "Would you like this reversal to refund the customer's credit card?" Yes refunds the card. No leaves the card alone ("Do not refund the card"). Refund the customer another way if needed.
  • Receipts On Account: Removing a card payment asks you to confirm the void at your processor.
  • Customer Deposits: Reversing a deposit paid by card voids or refunds the card and shows the refunded amount and authorization number.
  • Refund Unused Amount: On a partly used deposit, add the refund payments, then select Save.

When Store Account doesn't show

The Store Account payment type is hidden when:

  • The customer is a business customer with a $0 credit limit (Customer Maintenance, General tab).
  • The customer is past due. Cash, Credit Card, Credit Card on File, Check, and Customer Deposits stay available.
